Parametric forms

δ(N,s) ≜ ln(D/D₀) = α · N^β · s^b s*(N,D*) = [ln(D*/D₀(N)) / (α · N^β)]^(1/b) R*(N,D*) = N · (1 − s*) s_env(N,D*) = 1 − R̂(D*)/N

WikiText2: ln(D/D0) = 17.929 · N^(-0.397) · s^5.03 · D0(N) = 5.59 + 10.67 · N^(-0.603)

PTB: ln(D/D0) = 16.108 · N^(-0.364) · s^4.77 · D0(N) = 6.60 + 25.00 · N^(-0.521)

Asymptotics under the fit: 1−s* ∼ N^m with m≈−0.24…−0.34, while 1−s_env ∼ N^−1. Envelope rate is not distortion-feasible for large N. Extrapolation beyond 27B is indicative only.
